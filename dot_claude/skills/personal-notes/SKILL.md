## Personal notes locations

Notes are stored in two locations:

- **Obsidian vault** (primary, actively used): `~/Library/Mobile Documents/iCloud~md~obsidian/Documents/Obsidian Notes`
- **`~/Projects/notes`** (legacy, migrating away from): notes being migrated into Obsidian

When looking up or referencing notes, prefer the Obsidian vault first. Also search `~/Projects/notes` as it may contain notes not yet migrated.

> **Note:** For creating posts (called notes) on micahsvenson.com, use the `create-post` skill instead.
